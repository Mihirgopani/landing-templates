How is Vault Fitness different from a regular gym? +

Unlike commercial gyms, Vault is an exclusive, appointment-only studio. You won't wait for equipment, deal with crowds, or navigate a sea of distractions. Every minute of your session is coached and optimized for your specific goals.

Do I need to be fit to join? +

Not at all. Our personal training programs are scaled to your current fitness level. Whether you're a complete beginner or an advanced athlete, we engineer the protocol to meet you exactly where you are.

Where exactly are you located? +

We are located on the 3rd floor of A-40, Chabra Rd, near the BJP office in Hawa Sadak, Ramnagar, Jaipur. It's easily accessible with ample parking nearby.

Do you provide diet and nutrition plans? +

Yes. Training is only half the equation. We provide comprehensive nutrition architecture, including custom macro calculations, meal timing strategies, and supplement guidance tailored to your objectives.

How do I book a session or tour? +
