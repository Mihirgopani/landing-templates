What is the process for getting dental implants?

The process begins with a comprehensive 3D CBCT scan to assess your bone density. Dr. Desai will then design a customized treatment plan. The implant is placed under local anesthesia (painless), followed by a healing period of 3-6 months. Finally, a custom crown is attached to restore your smile.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes, we accommodate emergency walk-ins for severe toothaches, trauma, or sudden oral issues. Please call us at +91 97139 07140 so we can prepare for your immediate arrival and minimize your wait time.

Is the clinic equipped with advanced sterilization protocols?

Absolutely. Patient safety is our utmost priority. We use hospital-grade B-Class autoclaves for sterilizing all instruments, employ single-use disposable kits for every patient, and maintain strict cross-contamination prevention protocols.

Where exactly is the clinic located in Indore?

We are located at 40, Sahid Bhagat Singh Society, on Sapna Sangeeta Road, right behind Inox and opposite Nidan Pathology in Ashok Nagar. The area offers ample parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers.

Do you offer EMI options for expensive treatments?

Yes, we understand that advanced treatments like implants and full-mouth rehabilitations are a significant investment. We offer flexible payment plans and no-cost EMI options on major credit cards to make your care accessible.
