How far in advance should we book Lensation Studio?

Since we limit the number of weddings we cover each year to ensure premium quality, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance. However, feel free to reach out for last-minute availability!

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! We love traveling and have covered beautiful destination weddings across India and abroad. Travel and accommodation details can be discussed during your consultation.

How long does it take to receive the final album and film?

We deliver a sneak peek of 30-50 edited images within a week of your wedding. The complete cinematic film and the final curated premium album typically take 8 to 12 weeks, as we meticulously edit every frame.

What is included in your wedding packages?

Our packages are fully customizable based on your events and needs. They generally include multiple shooters, cinematic drone coverage, premium editing, a hardcover luxury album, and high-resolution digital copies.

Where is your studio located in Nagpur?

We are located at Flat No 308, Anandsagar Appt, 2, New Mankapur, Nagpur. You can visit us by appointment to discuss your vision and view our physical portfolios.
