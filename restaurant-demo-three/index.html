Our Philosophy

Rooted in Tradition

At Olive & Vine, we believe that the best meals are born from the best ingredients. We partner directly with local farmers and sustainable fisheries to bring the vibrant flavors of the Mediterranean coast to your table.

Our wood-fired oven is the heart of our kitchen, imparting a unique, smoky depth to our breads, vegetables, and meats that cannot be replicated.
