Our Journey

From the Coast to Your Table

A Labor of Love

Founded in 2015 by Executive Chef Marco Rossi, Olive & Vine was born from a simple yet profound belief: food should transport you. Growing up in a small coastal town in Italy, Marco learned that the secret to extraordinary food lies in respecting the ingredients.

Today, we uphold that tradition by sourcing heirloom tomatoes from local valleys, fresh daily catches from sustainable fisheries, and importing our extra virgin olive oil and aged balsamic directly from family estates in Modena and Tuscany.
