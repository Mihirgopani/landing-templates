Our Journey

From Humble Beginnings to Culinary Landmark

Founded in 2018, Lumière Bistro began with a simple vision: to create a dining experience that honors the rich traditions of classic French cuisine while embracing the vibrant, local flavors of our region.

What started as a small, 20-seat neighborhood bistro has grown into a celebrated culinary landmark, thanks to the unwavering support of our community and our team's dedication to excellence.

Meet The Chef

Executive Chef Antoine Moreau

"Cooking is not just about feeding people; it's about creating memories that linger long after the last bite."

With over 20 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Paris and Lyon, Chef Moreau brings a wealth of knowledge and a deeply personal touch to every dish at Lumière. His philosophy centers on letting high-quality, seasonal ingredients speak for themselves, enhanced by precise, modern techniques.

Under his leadership, Lumière Bistro has been recognized with the "Best New Restaurant" award and maintains a coveted spot on the city's top dining lists.
