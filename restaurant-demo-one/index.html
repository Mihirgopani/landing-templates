Welcome to Lumière

Where Tradition Meets Innovation

At Lumière Bistro, dining is not just about sustenance; it is an experience. Founded by Executive Chef Antoine Moreau, our kitchen is a canvas where seasonal, locally sourced ingredients are transformed into modern masterpieces.

From our hand-selected wine pairings to our meticulously plated desserts, every detail is designed to transport you.
