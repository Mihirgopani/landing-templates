Chat with Us
Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

While we welcome walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment via WhatsApp or call to guarantee your preferred time slot and stylist, especially on weekends.

What are your operating hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 9:30 PM. We recommend booking your slot in advance to ensure a seamless and relaxed experience without any wait times.

Do you offer services for both men and women?

Absolutely. Signature is a premier unisex salon. We offer a comprehensive range of services tailored for men, women, and everyone in between, from precision cuts to luxury spa therapies.

Is parking available at Shubhlaxmi Complex?

Yes, Shubhlaxmi Complex on City Light Road offers ample and convenient parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, making your visit completely hassle-free.

What hygiene protocols do you follow?

Your safety is our priority. We strictly adhere to hospital-grade sterilization for all tools, use single-use disposables wherever applicable, and maintain immaculate cleanliness across all stations.
