More than pictures.
We archive feelings.

At Thakur's Film City, we believe a wedding isn't just an event; it's a fleeting masterpiece of emotions, traditions, and raw human connection.

Based in the heart of Nagpur on Katol Road, our team of visual storytellers blends the nostalgic warmth of analog film aesthetics with modern cinematic precision. We step back, observe, and capture the unscripted magic of your day, ensuring your album feels less like a portfolio and more like a time capsule.

◉ INDEX / INQUIRIES

Frequently asked.

Planning a wedding can be overwhelming. Let us make the photography part seamless and stress-free.

Ask Us Anything
Q: How far in advance should we book? +

A: Since we limit the number of weddings we cover each year to ensure premium quality, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance. However, feel free to reach out for last-minute availability!

Q: Do you travel for destination weddings? +

A: Absolutely! We love traveling and have covered beautiful destination weddings across India and abroad. Travel and accommodation details can be discussed during your consultation.

Q: When do we get the final album? +

A: We deliver a sneak peek of 30-50 edited images within a week. The complete cinematic film and the final curated premium album typically take 8 to 12 weeks, as we meticulously edit every frame.

Q: Where is your studio located? +

A: We are located at PLOT NO. 203, Katol Rd, Pension Nagar, Police Line Takli, Nagpur. You can visit us by appointment to discuss your vision and view our physical portfolios.
