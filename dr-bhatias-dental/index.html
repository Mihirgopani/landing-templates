Common Inquiries

Everything you
need to know.

Do you treat both dental and ENT issues?

Yes. Dento Facial Square is a unique multi-specialty clinic. We have dedicated departments and specialists for comprehensive dental care (cosmetic, implants, orthodontics) as well as advanced Ear, Nose, and Throat (ENT) treatments.

Is the clinic equipped for emergency dental care?

Absolutely. We accommodate emergency walk-ins for severe toothaches, trauma, or sudden ENT issues. Please call us at +91 731 356 5839 so we can prepare for your immediate arrival.

What safety and sterilization protocols do you follow?

Patient safety is our utmost priority. We use hospital-grade autoclaves for sterilizing all instruments, employ single-use disposable kits for every patient, and maintain strict cross-contamination prevention protocols.

Is parking available at Pearl Business Park?

Yes, Pearl Business Park on A.B Road offers ample and convenient parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, ensuring a hassle-free visit for our patients.
