What are the membership plans and pricing?

We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and annual membership plans to suit every budget and commitment level. We also have specialized packages for personal training and group classes. Reach out on WhatsApp for our current pricing and any ongoing offers.

Do you provide personal training?

Yes! Our certified personal trainers offer one-on-one coaching with customized workout plans, form correction, progress tracking, and nutrition guidance. We have trainers specialized in strength, weight loss, and sports performance.

What are your operating hours?

We're open 7 days a week from 5:30 AM to 10:30 PM, including all weekends and most holidays. This flexible schedule ensures you can train whenever it fits your lifestyle — early morning or late evening.

Is there parking available at V Cube?

Yes, V Cube has dedicated parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers. Being located opposite Gaurav Tower on Malviya Nagar's main Marg, we're also easily accessible by public transport.

Do you have women-specific facilities?

Absolutely. We have separate, clean, and well-maintained changing rooms and washrooms for women. We also run dedicated women-only batch timings and specialized Zumba & yoga classes to ensure a comfortable environment.

Can I try before I join?

Of course! We offer a free trial session so you can experience our facility, meet our trainers, and see if Lifetime Fitness Club is the right fit for you. Just WhatsApp us to book your free visit.
