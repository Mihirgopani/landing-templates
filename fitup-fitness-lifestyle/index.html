06 Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Can't find your answer? Our team is just a WhatsApp message away.

Chat with Us
What membership plans do you offer?

We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and annual membership plans designed to suit every commitment level. We also have specialized packages for personal training, group classes, and wellness programs. Reach out on WhatsApp for our current pricing and any ongoing introductory offers.

Do you provide personal training sessions?

Absolutely. Our certified personal trainers offer bespoke one-on-one coaching with customized workout plans, form correction, progress tracking, and complete nutrition guidance. We have specialists in strength training, weight loss, and athletic performance.

What are your operating hours?

We're open 7 days a week from 5:30 AM to 10:30 PM, including weekends and most holidays. This flexible schedule ensures you can train whenever it fits your lifestyle — whether you're an early riser or prefer evening sessions.

Is parking available at Bhagadiya Bhawan?

Yes, Bhagadiya Bhawan offers dedicated parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers. Located on the prominent Subhash Marg in C Scheme, we're easily accessible from all major parts of Jaipur including Civil Lines, Ashok Nagar, and Raja Park.

Do you have women-specific facilities and timings?

Yes, we have separate, clean, and well-maintained changing rooms for women. We also offer dedicated women-only batch timings and specialized programs including yoga, Zumba, and strength training in a comfortable, empowering environment.

Can I try before committing to a membership?

Of course! We offer a complimentary trial session so you can experience our facility, meet our trainers, and see if Fitup Fitness Lifestyle is the right fit for you. Simply WhatsApp us to book your free visit — no obligations.
