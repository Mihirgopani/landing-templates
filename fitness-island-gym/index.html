What are the membership options at fitness island?

We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, and annual membership plans tailored to your commitment level. We also provide specialized packages for personal training and group sessions. Reach out to us for our current pricing and any ongoing introductory offers.

Do you provide certified personal training?

Absolutely. Our personal trainers are certified experts who design bespoke workout and nutrition plans. They focus on biomechanics, progressive overload, and injury prevention to ensure you achieve your goals safely and efficiently.

Are there specific timings for women?

Yes, we have dedicated women-only batch timings to ensure a comfortable and empowering environment. We also offer specialized programs tailored specifically for women's fitness goals, including strength, toning, and flexibility.

Is parking available at the facility?

Yes, our location on Nehru Path offers ample dedicated parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers. We ensure a hassle-free arrival experience for all our members.

What makes fitness island different from other gyms?

Our focus on a curated, premium environment. From our imported biomechanical equipment and pristine hygiene standards to our elite coaching staff and respectful community, every detail is designed to elevate your fitness journey beyond the ordinary.
