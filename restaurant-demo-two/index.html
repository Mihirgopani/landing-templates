Our Philosophy

Rooted in Excellence

Founded in 2018 by Executive Chef Elena Rostova, Aurelia was born from a singular vision: to create a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. We believe that true luxury lies in the details.

We partner directly with local farmers, sustainable fisheries, and artisanal producers to ensure that every ingredient on your plate is of the highest caliber.
