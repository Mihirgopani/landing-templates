Our Philosophy

Rooted in Excellence

A Symphony of Flavor

Founded in 2018 by Executive Chef Elena Rostova, Aurelia was born from a singular vision: to create a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. We believe that true luxury lies in the details.

We partner directly with local farmers, sustainable fisheries, and artisanal producers to ensure that every ingredient on your plate is of the highest caliber. Our kitchen is a sanctuary of creativity, where classical techniques meet modern innovation.

Our Values

Sustainability

Zero-waste kitchen practices and a steadfast commitment to ethical, local sourcing.

Craftsmanship

Meticulous attention to detail in every recipe, technique, and visual presentation.

Hospitality

Anticipatory, warm, and unobtrusive service that makes every guest feel truly valued.

The Team

Meet Chef Elena Rostova

With over 15 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Paris and Lyon, Chef Elena brings a wealth of knowledge and a deeply personal touch to every dish at Aurelia.

Her philosophy centers on letting high-quality, seasonal ingredients speak for themselves, enhanced by precise, modern techniques and an unwavering respect for the earth.
