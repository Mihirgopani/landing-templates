The Art of Our Sourdough

At Aurelia, bread is not an afterthought; it is the foundation of the meal. Our signature sourdough is a labor of love, requiring a meticulous 48-hour fermentation process that develops its characteristic tang and airy crumb.

The Starter

It all begins with "Mother," our sourdough starter that has been alive and thriving in our kitchen for over five years. Fed daily with organic whole wheat flour and filtered water, it is the living heart of our bakery.

"Bread is a feeling. It's the warmth of the oven, the smell of fermentation, and the satisfaction of sharing something made entirely by hand."

The Process

After an initial bulk fermentation, the dough is gently folded to build structure without deflating the natural gases. It is then divided, shaped, and left to proof in the refrigerator overnight. This cold retardation is the secret to our complex flavor profile.

Baked at high temperatures in our stone hearth oven, the result is a loaf with a shatteringly crisp crust and a soft, open interior. We serve it warm, with cultured butter and flaky sea salt, to every table.
