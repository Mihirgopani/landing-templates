A5 Wagyu Tenderloin

$85

An unparalleled culinary experience. Our authentic Japanese A5 Wagyu is lightly seared over binchotan charcoal to preserve its delicate marbling, melting in your mouth. Served alongside a silky black truffle pomme purée, roasted bone marrow, and a rich, reduced Cabernet Sauvignon sauce.

Gluten-Free Dairy-Free Option
Reserve a Table to Enjoy This

"The secret to this dish is restraint. The Wagyu speaks for itself; our role is merely to elevate its natural richness with the earthy depth of black truffle and the acidity of a fine wine reduction."

— Executive Chef Elena Rostova
Premium Ingredients

Miyazaki Prefecture A5 Wagyu Beef, Yukon Gold Potatoes, Périgord Black Winter Truffle, Beef Bone Marrow, Shallots, Fresh Thyme, Rosemary, Premium Cabernet Sauvignon.

Allergen Information
Contains: Dairy (Butter, Cream), Sulfites (Wine).
Please note: Prepared in a kitchen that handles gluten, nuts, and shellfish. Please inform your server of any severe allergies.

Sommelier Pairing Recommendation

Our Head Sommelier recommends pairing this dish with our 2018 Château Margaux or a bold, structured Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon to beautifully complement the rich marbling and savory notes of the Wagyu.
