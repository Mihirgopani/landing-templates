About Us

A trust built on small starts and steady steps.

Founded to give underserved children and families a real first step forward, Aarambh Charitable Trust runs grassroots programs in education, healthcare access and skill-building — because lasting change is walked, one footprint at a time, with a community behind every child.

  • ✓ 100% of public donations go directly to field programs
  • ✓ Registered charitable trust with full transparency reporting
  • ✓ On-ground teams across 6 districts
  • ✓ Verified by independent annual audits
