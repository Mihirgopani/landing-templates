Navigating financial regulations can be complex. We have compiled answers to the most common questions we receive from our clients.

Ask a Question
How can R D Bhatti & Associates help my business grow?

We act as your strategic financial partners. By optimizing your tax liabilities, ensuring strict compliance to avoid penalties, and providing data-driven financial insights, we free up your resources to focus entirely on scaling your core business operations.

Do you provide specialized services for NRI clients?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated desk for NRI taxation. We assist with Indian income tax filing, TDS exemptions, repatriation of funds, and tax implications on property sales or investments in India, ensuring full compliance with FEMA and Income Tax Act regulations.

What is the process for GST registration and compliance?

We handle the entire lifecycle. From determining your liability and gathering documentation to filing the application on the GST portal. Post-registration, we manage your monthly/quarterly return filings, reconciliation of GSTR-2A/2B, and annual returns to ensure zero disruptions.

Is my financial data kept strictly confidential?

Uncompromising confidentiality is the cornerstone of our practice. As Chartered Accountants, we are bound by a strict code of ethics. Your financial data, business strategies, and personal information are secured with the highest levels of privacy and data protection protocols.
