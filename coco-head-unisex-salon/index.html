Chat with Us
Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

While we welcome walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment via WhatsApp or call to guarantee your preferred time slot and stylist, especially on weekends.

What are your operating hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. We recommend booking your slot in advance to ensure a seamless and relaxed experience without any wait times.

Do you offer specialized bridal packages?

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive bridal and pre-bridal couture packages including HD/Airbrush makeup, hair styling, mehendi ceremonies, and full-body pampering. Please reach out for a customized consultation.

Is parking available at the salon?

Yes, Atlanta Business Hub on VIP Road offers ample and convenient parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, making your visit completely hassle-free.

What hygiene protocols do you follow?

Your safety is our priority. We strictly adhere to hospital-grade sterilization for all tools, use single-use disposables wherever applicable, and maintain immaculate cleanliness across all stations and spa rooms.
