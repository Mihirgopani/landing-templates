Patient Inquiries

Frequently
Asked Questions.

Navigating international healthcare can be complex. Our dedicated patient liaisons are available 24/7 to assist you.

Speak to a Liaison
How does the medical concierge service work?

From your initial inquiry, a dedicated liaison is assigned to you. They coordinate your medical records transfer, schedule virtual consultations, arrange your travel and luxury accommodation in Geneva, and organize private airport transfers. Your concierge remains your single point of contact throughout your entire journey.

Do you assist with medical visas for Switzerland?

Yes. Our administrative team provides all necessary official medical documentation, invitation letters, and clinic accreditation certificates required to support your Swiss medical visa application, ensuring a smooth and expedited process.

What languages do your physicians speak?

Our international medical team and concierge staff are fluent in English, French, German, Arabic, Mandarin, and Russian, ensuring seamless communication and absolute clarity regarding your care plan.

How is my privacy and confidentiality maintained?

Privacy is the cornerstone of our practice. We operate under strict Swiss patient confidentiality laws. Our clinic features private, discreet entrances, VIP suites with enhanced security, and encrypted, blockchain-secured medical records. We routinely host high-profile individuals and dignitaries.
