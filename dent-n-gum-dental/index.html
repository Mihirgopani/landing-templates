Is the treatment really painless? +

Yes. Dr. Akshat Sharma specializes in painless dentistry. We use advanced local anesthesia techniques, topical gels, and a gentle approach to ensure you feel zero discomfort during procedures like root canals and extractions.

Do I need to book an appointment? +

While we accommodate emergency walk-ins, we highly recommend booking an appointment via WhatsApp or call. This guarantees your preferred time slot and minimizes your wait time at the clinic.

What safety and hygiene protocols do you follow? +

Patient safety is our top priority. We use hospital-grade autoclaves for sterilization, single-use disposable kits for every patient, and UV sterilization chambers. Our clinic adheres to the strictest medical hygiene standards.

Is parking available at the clinic? +

Yes, our location at 344 A, near Mahalaxmi Gate in Vijay Nagar offers convenient and ample parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, making your visit completely hassle-free.

Do you treat children? +

Absolutely. We have a dedicated, gentle approach to pediatric dentistry. Our team is trained to make children feel comfortable and safe, turning dental visits into a positive experience from an early age.
