What are the membership plans and pricing?

We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, and annual membership plans to suit your commitment level. We also have specialized packages for personal training. Reach out via WhatsApp for our current pricing and any ongoing introductory offers.

Is the gym really open 24/7?

Yes! As an Anytime Fitness club, we are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Members have secure, keyless fob access to the facility at any time, day or night.

Do you provide personal training?

Absolutely. Our certified personal trainers offer one-on-one coaching with customized workout plans, form correction, progress tracking, and complete nutrition guidance to accelerate your results.

Is parking available at Pavilion?

Yes, the Pavilion building on Tonk Road offers ample dedicated parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers, making your visit hassle-free.

Can I use my membership at other locations?

Yes! One of the biggest benefits of Anytime Fitness is our global access. Your membership allows you to work out at any of our thousands of clubs worldwide.
