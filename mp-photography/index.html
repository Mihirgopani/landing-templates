How far in advance should we book your services?

We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 months in advance for weddings and large events to ensure we are available on your preferred date. For portrait sessions, 2 to 3 weeks is usually sufficient.

Do you travel for destination weddings or shoots?

Absolutely! We love traveling and capturing love stories across beautiful locations. Destination weddings and pre-wedding shoots are available across India and abroad. Travel expenses can be discussed during the consultation.

How long does it take to receive the final photos and videos?

We know you are excited to see your photos! We typically deliver a sneak peek within a week. The complete edited gallery and cinematic film usually take 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the size of the event.

Where is your studio located in Nagpur?

Our studio is conveniently located on Dayanand Park Road, beside the Unique Car Washing Centre and Medplus Pharmacy. You are welcome to visit us by appointment to discuss your vision and view our albums.
